How It Works

From facility registration to generating inspection-ready records, see how EcoTrace integrates into your daily yard workflow.

1
🏗️

Onboarding

Facility registration and document setup. Our team maps your yard's specific processes, capacities, and active licenses to tailor the system to your operations.

2
📱

Daily Recording

Simple activity confirmations. Weighbridge operators and floor managers log inward receipts, daily processing runs, and material dispatch with basic taps and photo proof.

3
🔍

Monitoring

The system checks completeness continuously. It verifies mass balances in real-time and alerts you instantly if quantities don't match or required records are missing.

4
🗂️

Documentation

Automatic registers and reports are assembled in the background. Your daily entries instantly update statutory stock ledgers, processing registers, and formatted summaries.

5
✅

Compliance Use

Clean documents are finally used in official reporting. Hand over perfectly formatted, verifiable records to inspectors, PROs, or Pollution Control boards without any last-minute stress.
