How EcoTrace Works

A step-by-step compliant process.

1

Record Activity

Simple tap and photo at the facility whenever waste arrives, is processed, or is dispatched.

2

System Builds Records

EcoTrace automatically populates stock registers and processing logs based on the recorded activity.

3

Inspection Files Generated

Date-wise logs and printable files are kept ready for any surprise or planned inspections.

4

Data Structured

Material history, timestamp records, and photo proofs are cross-verified and stored securely.

5

Usable for Compliance Reporting

Monthly summaries are exported in formats accepted by compliance agencies and PROs.
